# Airline Flight Operations – Key Insights

## 1. Flight Status Distribution
The analysis shows that most flights are successfully completed, while a smaller portion experience delays or cancellations. Monitoring flight status helps measure operational efficiency and identify areas for improvement.

## 2. Delayed vs On-Time Flights
Delayed flights represent a noticeable share of total operations, indicating opportunities to improve scheduling, turnaround times, and airport coordination to enhance punctuality.

## 3. High-Traffic Routes
Certain departure and arrival airport pairs have significantly higher flight volumes. These high-traffic routes are critical for network planning and may require additional capacity or operational focus.

## 4. Monthly Flight Trends
Flight volumes vary across months, highlighting seasonality in air travel. Understanding these trends supports better planning for staffing, fleet allocation, and maintenance schedules.

## 5. Aircraft Usage Analysis
Some aircraft types are used more frequently than others, indicating their importance in daily operations. This insight helps optimize fleet utilization and maintenance planning.
